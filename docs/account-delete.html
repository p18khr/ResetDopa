Delete Your ResetDopa Account

This page lets you permanently delete your ResetDopa account and associated personal data. Deletion is irreversible — please read the details below before continuing.

What will be deleted

  • Your profile and account identifiers (email, display name).
  • User-generated content and personal settings (timers, goals, preferences).
  • Device tokens and session data used for notifications and sync.

What may remain

  • Aggregate or anonymized analytics data.
  • Backups and logs for a short retention window (see timeline below).

How deletion works (recommended flow)

  1. Sign into the app or website with the account you want to delete.
  2. Request deletion from Settings → Delete Account (in-app) or click "Request deletion" below to receive a confirmation email.
  3. Confirm deletion from the email link (this prevents unauthorized deletion).
  4. We schedule and complete deletion within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email with a reference id when finished.

Request deletion

If you are signed in on this device, use the in-app Settings → Delete Account flow. If you prefer an email confirmation, click the button below to send a secure deletion link to your account email.

Timeline & recovery

Deletion is typically completed within 30 days. During this period you may contact support to request recovery (subject to identity verification), but after completion data cannot be restored.

Contact

If you have questions or need help, contact our privacy team: privacy@resetdopa.com

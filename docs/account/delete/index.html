Delete Your ResetDopa Account

Use this page to permanently delete your account and associated personal data. Deletion is irreversible once completed.

Security note: This page is public, but the actual deletion request is only accepted after account verification. The secure flow sends a one-time confirmation link to your email address.

What will be deleted

  • Your profile, email, display name, and account identifiers.
  • Personal settings, progress data, streaks, and logs tied to your account.
  • Device tokens and session data used for notifications and sync.

What may remain

  • Aggregated or anonymized analytics.
  • Backup copies and logs kept only for a short retention window.

How it works

  1. Sign into the app with the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap Request Account Deletion in Settings or use the button below.
  3. Confirm deletion from the emailed one-time link.
  4. We complete deletion within 30 days and email a reference ID when finished.
